Some of the most relaxed family sessions I photograph never leave the house. No packing the car, no racing the light at an unfamiliar park, no wrangling a toddler who'd rather be home anyway. You open the door, and we start.

If you've only ever pictured family photos as posed smiles against a studio backdrop, an at-home session is worth a closer look. Here's how in-home family photography works, why it tends to produce such natural images, and how to prepare for one. The photos throughout are from a recent at-home session in Burbank.

## Why at-home family sessions work

**Your home already looks like you.** A studio is a blank space you have to fill. Your house is full of the small things that make your family *yours*, the morning light through a particular window, the bed everyone piles onto, the backyard where the kids actually play. We don't have to manufacture a mood. It's already there.

**Kids are at their best when they're comfortable.** This is the big one, especially with babies and young children. At home, naps stay on schedule, snacks are in the fridge, and nobody's melting down because we drove across town. Comfortable kids give you real expressions instead of forced ones, and there's nowhere more comfortable than home.

**The images feel meaningful later.** The quiet, in-between frames, a baby passed from arms to arms, a big sister climbing in close, little hands, soft light, are the ones parents tell me they treasure most years later. Not the posed ones. The real ones. Home is where those moments naturally happen.

## Indoors and outdoors in one session

One of the advantages of shooting at home is range. We can start inside, where the light is soft and the mood is intimate, then move out to a backyard, porch, or nearby street for something brighter and more open.

That mix gives a gallery real variety, the same family, the same afternoon, two completely different feelings. You don't need a dramatic location. A tidy room with good window light and a bit of greenery outside is plenty.

## How to prepare for an at-home session

A little preparation goes a long way, and none of it is complicated:

- **Pick your best-light rooms.** The spaces with the most natural daylight photograph best. I'll help you identify them, but a quick tidy of one or two rooms is usually all it takes.
- **Dress comfortably and coordinate, don't match.** Choose colors and textures you feel most like yourselves in. Neutral and soft tones tend to work beautifully with indoor light. I send a full what-to-wear guide once we book.
- **Time it around your kids.** We schedule around naps and feedings so little ones are at their happiest. The best moments usually happen in the unstructured stretches, not the posed ones.
- **Leave the rest to me.** You don't need to stage your home or stress about perfection. The lived-in details are part of the story.

## Is an at-home session right for you?

In-home sessions are an especially good fit for families with new babies or young kids, anyone who finds leaving the house with little ones stressful, and parents who want photos that feel personal rather than generic. If your home has decent natural light, it's very likely a great candidate, and if you'd rather add a nearby park or favorite spot, we can fold that in too.

A growing family changes fast. An at-home session is one of the simplest ways to hold onto exactly how this stretch of life felt, in the place you were living it.
