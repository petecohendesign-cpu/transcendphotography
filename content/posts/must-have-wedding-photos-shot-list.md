A great wedding album isn't just a highlight reel of the big moments, it's a complete story of the day, told through both the milestones and the quiet details in between. Here are the 11 shots I make sure to capture at every wedding, and why each one matters.

## 1. The First Look

One of the most emotionally charged moments of the entire day. When partners see each other in their wedding attire for the first time, privately, before the ceremony, the reactions are completely unguarded. I always look for a secluded spot with good light and give couples a moment to just be together.

## 2. Bridal Party Portraits

Beyond the standard group lineup, the best bridal party shots lean candid, laughter between friends, a quiet moment before things get started. Individual portraits of each person honor the relationships that matter most to the couple.

## 3. The Vows

This is the emotional center of the ceremony. I'm watching both partners' faces, but also scanning the guests, a parent's expression, a sibling's reaction. Those peripheral moments often become some of the most meaningful photos in the album.

## 4. The Kiss

Photographed from multiple angles to capture both the couple and the crowd's reaction simultaneously. The official beginning of the marriage deserves more than one frame.

## 5. Reception Candids

The laughter, the dancing, the conversations at the edges of the room, these unposed moments give a wedding album its warmth and personality. I spend a lot of time at receptions just watching and waiting.

## 6. The Details

Dress lace, table settings, flowers, stationery, the rings before they go on, these are the personalized touches that tell your specific story. I capture these during the getting-ready window and during cocktail hour.

## 7. The First Dance

Shot from multiple perspectives: wide for atmosphere, close for emotion. The first dance is one of the few moments in the reception that's both intimate and center-stage.

## 8. Parent Dances

Often more emotionally weighted than couples anticipate. These dances mark a life transition, and the expressions, both the couple's and the parents', are worth capturing carefully.

## 9. The Cake Cutting

A lighter, celebratory moment that also produces great couple interaction. Guest reactions in the background add to it.

## 10. The Grand Exit

Sparklers, flower petals, streamers, whatever your exit looks like, it's one of the most visually dynamic moments of the night. With the right light and timing, these images are stunning.

## 11. The Full Group Shot

Every guest who attended your wedding, together in one frame. It's logistically the most challenging photo of the day, but it becomes a priceless record of everyone who celebrated with you.

---

Every wedding has its own rhythm and priorities. These 11 shots form the backbone of comprehensive coverage, but the best albums also include the unexpected moments no one planned for, and those are the ones I'm always watching for.
