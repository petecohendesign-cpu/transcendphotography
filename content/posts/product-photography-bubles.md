When Maya, the owner of Bubles Champagne, reached out about doing a brand and product shoot, I knew it was going to be something special. Her company has such a fun, West Coast vibe, built around the idea of celebrating life’s moments with a glass of champagne in hand. Bubles offers curated gift boxes, custom event setups, and their signature champagne carts that show up at weddings and parties all around Southern California.

You can tell right away that Maya has put her heart into creating a brand that feels elevated yet approachable. It’s stylish, polished, and totally rooted in that easygoing SoCal energy.

The Vision Behind the Shoot

For this session, we wanted to tell the full story of what it feels like to receive one of these champagne gift boxes. We also wanted to capture the different sizes and styles that Bubles offers, from smaller boxes perfect for personal gifts to larger options ideal for corporate gifting or events.

Our goal was to blend clean product photography with lifestyle storytelling that shows the personality of the brand. It wasn’t just about the products themselves, but the feeling of excitement that comes when you open a beautifully packaged gift and find champagne waiting inside.

The Perfect Location

We shot everything at Maya’s beautiful home in Montecito Heights. Her patio overlooks the Los Angeles skyline, which gave us the perfect mix of natural light and city energy. The setting matched the brand perfectly: elegant, relaxed, and full of that West Coast charm.

The house had so many great details to work with. We used a mix of indoor and outdoor setups, taking advantage of the sunny afternoon light on the patio. It created the right balance between polished product photos and warm, inviting lifestyle shots.

Mixing Product and Lifestyle Photography

We started the day by photographing the champagne gift boxes in a clean, minimal style. The focus was on texture, packaging, and presentation. Every ribbon, bottle, and logo was captured clearly so potential customers could really see the quality of what Bubles offers.

Once we had those foundation shots done, we moved into lifestyle scenes. This part was all about showing the experience. We styled a few setups that captured moments like:

The box waiting at your doorstep, sunlight hitting the packaging just right

A hand reaching out from the doorway to bring the package inside

A close-up look at the box before it’s opened, highlighting the elegant wrapping and details

The unboxing moment, with tissue being pulled back to reveal the champagne bottle and curated treats inside

A few relaxed lifestyle shots on the patio, where the boxes sat beside champagne glasses and flowers, bringing that warm, celebratory feel to life

By mixing product and lifestyle photos, we were able to show both sides of the brand: the craftsmanship of the boxes themselves and the emotion of the gifting experience.

Capturing the Brand’s Personality

Everything about Bubles feels joyful, polished, and a little indulgent in the best way. The champagne boxes are meant to be shared, whether you’re gifting a friend, a client, or celebrating something special yourself.

Maya’s business isn’t just about selling champagne. It’s about helping people create moments that feel thoughtful and elevated. On their website, Bubles describes themselves as a company that creates memorable champagne experiences, bringing people together to celebrate life with style. After seeing it all up close, that description couldn’t be more accurate.

The photos we created give customers a clear look at what Bubles has to offer. You can see how much care goes into every box, from the selection of the items inside to the way it’s presented. The images also show how versatile these gifts are, whether for personal celebrations or professional events.

The Champagne Cart

This wasn’t the first time Maya and I worked together. We recently did a

styled shoot

for her champagne carts, which she brings to weddings, events, and private parties. Those carts are always a hit, and they tie perfectly into the brand story we built with this photo session. Including them in a few shots helped show how the boxes and event services fit together into one seamless experience.

Why Great Brand Photography Matters

Strong visuals tell the story of a business in seconds. For a brand like Bubles Champagne, photography plays a huge role in communicating quality and style. The clean product shots are perfect for e-commerce and print, while the lifestyle photos are ideal for social media, email campaigns, and marketing materials.

If you’re a small business owner, this kind of photo session can help you build consistency across your website, Instagram, and promotional materials. When people can visualize themselves using your product or service, it creates a connection and makes your brand more memorable.

The Final Collection

By the end of the day, we had a full gallery that captured every side of the Bubles Champagne story. The crisp product photos show the craftsmanship, the lifestyle photos show the emotion, and the mix of both makes the brand feel alive.

This shoot was about more than just pretty packaging. It was about showcasing what it feels like to open something beautiful and experience a little bit of luxury, no matter the occasion.
